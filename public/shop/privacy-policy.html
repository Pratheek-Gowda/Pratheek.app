Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026

At Pratheek's Gesture, accessible from https://Pratheek.app/shop, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Pratheek's Gesture and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our services to order a new SIM or port your number, we may ask you to provide certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you. This includes:

  • Full Name
  • Phone Number
  • Delivery Location / Address
  • Email Address

Note: We do not collect highly sensitive government ID numbers (like Aadhar) directly through our website forms. ID verification is handled securely offline or in-person during SIM delivery.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our telecom services.
  • Process and deliver your SIM card orders and porting requests.
  • Communicate with you regarding your order status.
  • Understand and analyze how you use our website.

3. Google AdSense and DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our website and other sites on the internet. However, visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL – https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Third-Party Privacy Policies

Pratheek's Gesture's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information.

5. Consent
