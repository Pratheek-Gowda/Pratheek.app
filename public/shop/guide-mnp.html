The Ultimate Guide to MNP: How to Port Your Number Easily

In today's fast-paced digital world, staying connected is non-negotiable. Whether you are a student attending online classes, a professional taking client calls, or simply someone who loves streaming high-definition content, having a reliable mobile network is crucial. However, we've all been in situations where our current service provider drops the ball. Call drops, terrible indoor coverage, and slow internet speeds can be incredibly frustrating. The good news? You don't have to change your mobile number to change your network. This is where Mobile Number Portability (MNP) comes in.

What is MNP (Mobile Number Portability)?

Mobile Number Portability, commonly known as MNP, is a facility provided by telecom operators in India that allows you to switch from your current mobile network to a different one without having to change your 10-digit mobile number. Whether you want to move from Vodafone Idea (VI) to Airtel, BSNL to Jio, or any other combination, MNP ensures you keep your identity intact while upgrading your service quality.

Did you know? As per the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) guidelines, the porting process within the same telecom circle usually takes just 3 to 5 working days.

Prerequisites for Porting Your SIM

Before you begin the porting process, there are a few simple conditions you need to meet to ensure your request isn't rejected by your current operator:

  • Active Duration: You must have been using your current network operator for at least 90 days. If you recently bought a new SIM or ported a month ago, you cannot port again immediately.
  • Clear Dues: If you are a postpaid user, ensure all your pending bills are paid. For prepaid users, you don't need to worry about bills, but note that your existing balance will not carry over to the new network.
  • No Pending Legal Issues: Your number must not be involved in any pending legal or ownership disputes.

Step-by-Step Guide to the MNP Process

Ready to make the switch? Here is the exact, fail-proof method to port your number seamlessly in 2026:

Step 1: Generate the Unique Porting Code (UPC)

The first and most important step is to generate a UPC. Open your mobile's SMS app and type the word PORT followed by a space, and then your 10-digit mobile number. Send this SMS to the toll-free number 1900. (Example: PORT 9876543210).

Within a few minutes, you will receive an SMS from 1901 containing your 8-digit alphanumeric Unique Porting Code. Note that this code is usually valid for 4 days (or 30 days in specific regions like Jammu & Kashmir, Assam, and North East).

Step 2: Submit Your Request

Once you have your UPC, you can choose your new network. Through platforms like Pratheek's Gesture, you can simply fill out the Porting form for your desired network (Airtel, Jio, or VI). You will need to provide your basic contact details and your UPC. Alternatively, you can visit a local authorized retail store.

Step 3: Complete eKYC Verification

To finalize the connection, the government mandates KYC (Know Your Customer) verification. A representative will require your original Aadhar card for fingerprint or face verification. This process is fully digital and takes less than two minutes. Upon successful verification, you will be handed your new blank SIM card.

Step 4: Wait for the Switch

Your old SIM will continue to work normally for the next 3 to 5 days. You will receive an SMS notifying you of the exact date and time when the porting will happen (usually during midnight to avoid disruption). Once your old SIM loses network signal completely, remove it, insert your new SIM, and restart your phone. Welcome to your new network!
