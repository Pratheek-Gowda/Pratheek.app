How to Get a VIP or Fancy Mobile Number in India

Whether for a business trying to establish a memorable customer care hotline, or an individual looking for a status symbol, VIP and fancy mobile numbers are highly sought after in India. A customized number—such as one ending in 0000, mirroring your birthdate, or featuring lucky digits like 786—can make a strong impression. However, the market for VIP numbers is flooded with third-party brokers and scammers. In this guide, we will explain how to source and activate these numbers legally and directly from telecom operators.

SCAM WARNING: Never pay thousands of rupees to unverified Facebook or WhatsApp brokers claiming to have "exclusive" VIP numbers. They will often take your money and disappear, or port the number out after a few days. Always use official telecom portals.

Understanding Number Categories

Telecom operators classify premium numbers into different tiers, which dictate their price:

  • Free Custom Numbers: These are numbers where you can choose the last 4 to 6 digits based on your birth date, anniversary, or a simple pattern (like 1212 or 4545). These are usually offered for free or a nominal fee during a new postpaid connection sign-up.
  • Standard VIP Numbers: These feature minor repeating blocks (e.g., 98XX-55-66-77) or easy-to-remember sequences. They generally cost between ₹500 to ₹5,000.
  • Premium/Diamond Numbers: These are the elite status numbers featuring rare patterns like penta-digits (e.g., 98XXX-00000) or pure sequences (e.g., 9876543210). These are heavily guarded by operators and can be auctioned for anywhere from ₹10,000 to over ₹1 Lakh.

How to Book an Airtel VIP Number

Airtel allows users to search for customized numbers directly via their official website and the Airtel Thanks app.

  • Visit the official Airtel website and navigate to the 'Buy New Connection' section.
  • Select the 'Choose Your Number' option.
  • A search bar will appear allowing you to type in a sequence of 3 to 6 digits (like your birth year).
  • The system will display available numbers containing that sequence. Basic patterns are free with a new Postpaid plan, while premium patterns will display an upfront cost.
  • Once booked, an Airtel executive will visit your home for eKYC and deliver the SIM.

How to Book a Jio Vanity Number

Jio refers to customized sequences as "Choice Numbers."

  • Open the MyJio App or visit Jio.com.
  • Search for "Choice Number" in the top search bar.
  • Enter your preferred 4 to 5 digit sequence.
  • Jio will generate a list of available numbers. Currently, Jio charges a nominal booking fee (often around ₹499) for activating a choice number, which must be paid online during the booking process.
  • Complete the digital KYC to have the SIM activated.

How to Book a Vodafone Idea (Vi) Fancy Number

Vi has one of the most robust and publicly accessible VIP number portals among Indian telcos.

  • Visit the official myvi.in website and click on the 'Buy VIP Number' link in the top menu.
  • Vi categorizes numbers into 'Free' and 'Premium' tabs right on the dashboard.
  • You can search for a string of numbers or browse through their curated list of premium patterns.
  • If you select a premium number, you will pay the required fee online. Vi offers free doorstep delivery for all VIP number purchases.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I port my VIP number to another network?

Yes. A VIP number is treated exactly like a normal mobile number under TRAI regulations. Once you have activated it and passed the mandatory 90-day lock-in period, you can generate a UPC (Unique Porting Code) and use Mobile Number Portability (MNP) to switch to any other operator while keeping the VIP number.

Can I convert my VIP Postpaid number to Prepaid?

Yes. Operators usually force you to buy VIP numbers on Postpaid billing to ensure revenue. However, after the initial 90-day period (or the specific lock-in period mentioned in your contract), you can visit an operator gallery and request a migration from Postpaid to Prepaid.

Why can't I find the exact 10-digit number I want?

Because there are over a billion active mobile connections in India. The chances of a highly specific 10-digit sequence (like your exact birthdate plus birth year) being currently unregistered and available in the open pool are astronomically low. You must compromise and search for 4 to 6 digit matching blocks instead.
