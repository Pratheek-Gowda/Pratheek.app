The Definitive Guide to Mobile Number Portability (MNP) in India

In modern everyday life, your mobile number is far more than a communication channel—it is your digital identity. From banking OTPs and Unified Payments Interface (UPI) authentications to identity verifications and professional contacts, losing or changing your mobile number causes significant administrative headache. However, recurring network issues like weak indoor coverage, erratic call drops, or sub-par 4G/5G data speeds often make switching telecom service providers inevitable.

Fortunately, the Mobile Number Portability (MNP) framework in India allows subscribers to seamlessly switch service providers without altering their 10-digit telephone number. This detailed guide breaks down the legal eligibility criteria, the technical porting mechanism, regulatory timelines enforced by the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI), and common reasons porting requests get rejected.

What is Mobile Number Portability (MNP)?

Mobile Number Portability (MNP) is a telecom service framework governed by TRAI regulations that empowers mobile subscribers to retain their existing mobile telephone number when switching from one access provider to another, or from one service area (telecom circle) to another across India.

Whether you are migrating between prepaid and postpaid billing models or porting across different cellular technologies (such as upgrading from legacy 2G/4G connectivity to 5G Standalone networks), the porting infrastructure transfers your profile routing details across central clearinghouse systems without disconnecting your existing identity.

TRAI Regulatory Note: Under standard operating rules, intra-circle porting (switching operators within the same state/telecom circle) typically takes 3 to 5 working days, while inter-circle porting (migrating to a different state) completes within 5 working days.

Eligibility Criteria for Porting Your Number

Before initiating a porting request, verify that your connection fulfills all statutory conditions set forth by regulatory standards. Failure to meet any of these will trigger an immediate rejection code from your donor operator:

  • 90-Day Lock-in Period: Your mobile number must have been active on your current network for a minimum of 90 days. This applies both to brand-new SIM activations and previous porting cycles.
  • Prepaid vs. Postpaid Settlement: Postpaid subscribers must clear all outstanding invoices up to the current billing period, along with any unbilled cycle dues. Prepaid subscribers do not need billing clearances; however, any unused main balance, active validity days, or rollover data will expire upon porting and will not transfer.
  • Absence of Contractual Commitments: The connection must not be bound by legal holdbacks, ongoing contractual leasing terms, or court orders prohibiting number transfer.
  • Prohibition on Recent SIM Swaps: As a telecom security measure to counter unauthorized SIM-hijacking and financial fraud, porting requests are automatically blocked if the SIM card has undergone a SIM-swap or replacement within the previous 7 days.

Step-by-Step Porting Procedure

The MNP procedure is divided into four distinct phases: code generation, digital eKYC enrollment, backend verification, and final cutover.

Step 1: Generating the Unique Porting Code (UPC)

The Unique Porting Code (UPC) serves as the digital authorization token that authorizes the recipient operator to request control of your mobile routing profile from your current operator.

  1. Open the SMS messaging application on your phone.
  2. Type: PORT <10-digit mobile number> (For instance: PORT 9876543210).
  3. Send the message to the toll-free short code 1900.
  4. Within a few minutes, you will receive an automated response from short code 1901 containing your alphanumeric UPC alongside its exact expiration timestamp.

Validity Window: The UPC remains valid for 4 calendar days across all standard licensed service areas. In licensed service areas of Jammu & Kashmir, Assam, and the North East, the validity extends to 30 days.

Step 2: Submitting Documentation & Operator Selection

Once you possess an active UPC, you can select your target service provider based on regional coverage and competitive recharge tariffs. Step 3: Verification, Cutover, and Tele-Verification

After your digital enrollment is registered, your newly issued blank SIM card remains inactive while your donor operator validates records against clearance criteria. During this transitional phase, your existing SIM card continues to function normally with uninterrupted voice and data access.

Upon backend approval, you will receive an SMS confirmation detailing the scheduled cutover hour (typically scheduled between 11:00 PM and 5:00 AM to minimize daytime downtime). Once your old SIM displays "No Service", insert your new SIM, complete tele-verification or app confirmation as prompted by the operator, and normal service will resume.

Common MNP Rejection Reasons and Solutions

If your porting attempt encounters issues, review this quick diagnostic reference:

  • Rejection Code: Outstanding Balance (Postpaid): Ensure any unbilled usage charges since your last monthly invoice are paid in full before requesting your UPC.
  • Rejection Code: Under 90-Day Lifespan: Wait until the 91st calendar day following your last SIM registration before sending the UPC SMS to 1900.
  • Rejection Code: UPC Mismatch: Double-check the alphanumeric sequence. If the code expires before retail submission, send an SMS to 1900 again to generate a fresh token.
