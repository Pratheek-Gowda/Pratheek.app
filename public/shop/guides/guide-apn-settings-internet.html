The Ultimate Guide to APN Settings: Boost Your 4G/5G Speeds

Are you paying for high-speed 4G or 5G data but experiencing sluggish loading times, constant video buffering, or random connection drops? While network congestion is often to blame, one of the most overlooked solutions is optimizing your smartphone's APN (Access Point Name) settings. By manually configuring how your phone connects to the public internet, you can drastically improve latency (ping) and overall download speeds.

What is an APN and Why Does it Matter?

The Access Point Name (APN) is essentially the gateway between your cellular network and the public internet. It contains the network path, IP protocols, and security credentials your phone needs to request an IP address from your telecom operator (like Airtel, Jio, or Vi). When you insert a new SIM card, your phone automatically downloads a default APN profile. However, these default profiles are heavily generalized and may not be optimized for your specific region, device model, or the latest 5G bands.

By creating a custom APN, you can force your device to use faster routing servers and the most modern internet protocols (like IPv6), bypassing outdated default pathways.

How to Change APN Settings on Your Smartphone

For Android Devices:

  • Open your phone's Settings app.
  • Navigate to Network & Internet or Connections.
  • Tap on Mobile Networks -> Access Point Names.
  • Tap the '+' (Add) button or three dots in the top right corner to create a "New APN".
  • Input the specific settings for your operator listed below.
  • Tap Save, select the new APN to activate it, and restart your phone.

For iOS (iPhone) Devices:

  • Open the Settings app.
  • Tap on Mobile Service (or Cellular).
  • Tap on Mobile Data Network.
  • Note: Apple restricts manual APN editing for certain carriers in India if they push official carrier bundles. If you see the options, enter the details below. If not, your iPhone is locked to the operator's optimized bundle.

Best APN Settings for Reliance Jio (4G & 5G)

Jio operates a highly advanced, all-IP network. To get the best speeds out of Jio's 5G Standalone (SA) network, ensure your APN protocol is set to IPv6.

Name: Jio 5G Prime (or Jio 4G)

APN: jionet

Proxy / Port / Username / Password: Not Set

Server: www.google.com

MMSC / MMS Proxy / MMS Port: Not Set

MCC: 404 (Leave default)

MNC: Leave default (Changes by state)

Authentication Type: None

APN Type: default,supl,xcap

APN Protocol: IPv4/IPv6

APN Roaming Protocol: IPv4/IPv6

Bearer: Unspecified (Or select LTE and NR if specifically prompted)

Best APN Settings for Airtel

Airtel's network relies on Non-Standalone (NSA) 5G architecture, meaning it still uses the 4G core to establish connections. A stable APN is crucial here to prevent the phone from constantly dropping back to 4G.

Name: Airtel Internet

APN: airtelgprs.com

Proxy / Port / Username / Password: Not Set

Server: *99#

MCC / MNC: Leave default

Authentication Type: PAP or CHAP

APN Type: default,supl

APN Protocol: IPv4/IPv6

Bearer: LTE, NR

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Will changing the APN void my warranty or damage my phone?

Absolutely not. APN settings are just routing instructions. If you make a mistake and the internet stops working, you can simply press the "Reset to Default" button in your APN menu, and your phone will automatically download the correct standard settings again.

Why is my internet still slow after changing the APN?

APN settings optimize the software side of your connection. However, if the physical cell tower near your house is severely congested with thousands of users, or if you have a thick concrete wall blocking the signal, changing the APN will not magically fix hardware or physics-based limitations. In these cases, utilizing Wi-Fi Calling is recommended.

Should I change the MCC and MNC values?

No. MCC (Mobile Country Code) and MNC (Mobile Network Code) are automatically detected by your SIM card and identify your specific telecom circle (e.g., Karnataka, Delhi, Maharashtra). Changing these values will completely disconnect your phone from the network.
