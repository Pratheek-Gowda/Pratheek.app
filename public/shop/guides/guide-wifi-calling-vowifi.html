Fix Call Drops: How to Enable VoWiFi (Wi-Fi Calling)

Struggling with weak cellular network inside your home or office? Call drops, robotic voices, and unreachable statuses are common problems caused by thick walls blocking mobile tower signals. Fortunately, there is a built-in technology on modern smartphones that completely fixes this issue: VoWiFi (Voice over Wi-Fi), also known as Wi-Fi Calling.

What is Wi-Fi Calling?

Wi-Fi calling allows your smartphone to route standard cellular phone calls and SMS text messages over your broadband Wi-Fi connection instead of relying on the mobile cell tower. When you make a call, the audio data travels through your home internet router directly to the telecom operator's servers. This results in crystal-clear voice quality, zero call drops, and instant call connection times.

In India, Airtel, Reliance Jio, and Vodafone Idea (Vi) all support VoWiFi services across the country. Best of all, it is completely free to use—calls made over Wi-Fi are simply deducted from your existing plan's unlimited voice allowance.

How to Enable Wi-Fi Calling on iOS (iPhone)

Apple has deeply integrated Wi-Fi calling into iOS. To turn it on:

  • Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  • Scroll down and tap on Mobile Service (or Cellular).
  • Select your active SIM card (if you are using Dual SIM).
  • Tap on Wi-Fi Calling.
  • Toggle the switch for "Wi-Fi Calling on This iPhone" to the ON position.
  • Click "Enable" when the warning prompt appears. You will now see the word "Wi-Fi" next to your carrier name in the status bar when the feature is active.

How to Enable Wi-Fi Calling on Android

The exact wording might vary slightly depending on whether you are using a Samsung, Google Pixel, OnePlus, or Xiaomi device, but the general steps are identical:

  • Open the Settings app.
  • Navigate to Network & Internet or Connections.
  • Tap on Mobile Network or SIM Cards and select your active SIM.
  • Look for the option named Wi-Fi Calling or Make Calls using Wi-Fi.
  • Toggle the switch to ON.
  • Pro Tip: Look for a sub-setting called "Calling Preference" and set it to "Wi-Fi Preferred". This forces the phone to use Wi-Fi even if there is a weak cell tower nearby, preventing the phone from constantly switching between the two and dropping the call.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does Wi-Fi calling consume a lot of broadband data?

No, voice calls use very little data. A standard 5-minute Wi-Fi call will consume less than 5MB of internet data.

Can I use Wi-Fi calling on Airplane mode?

Yes! This is a great trick to ensure your phone doesn't try to connect to a weak cell tower. Turn on Airplane mode to disable the cellular antenna, then turn Wi-Fi back on. As long as you are connected to a router, you can make and receive standard phone calls and SMS.

Do I need a specific broadband provider?

No. Airtel Wi-Fi calling works over JioFiber, Jio Wi-Fi calling works over Airtel Xstream, and both work on local broadband providers like ACT, Hathway, or BSNL. Any stable broadband connection will work.
