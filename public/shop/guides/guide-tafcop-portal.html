TAFCOP Portal: How to Check How Many SIMs are on Your Aadhaar

Identity theft in the telecom sector is a growing concern. Cybercriminals and fraudsters frequently use stolen ID proofs to activate mobile numbers, which are then used for illegal activities, financial fraud, and spam. To combat this, the Department of Telecommunications (DoT), Government of India, launched the TAFCOP (Telecom Analytics for Fraud Management and Consumer Protection) portal, which is now integrated into the larger Sanchar Saathi initiative.

This incredibly vital public tool allows any citizen of India to check exactly how many mobile connections are officially issued against their name and Aadhaar card across all operators (Airtel, Jio, Vi, and BSNL). Here is your step-by-step guide to using the portal and securing your digital identity.

How to Check Your Registered SIM Connections

Checking your registered connections is entirely free and takes less than two minutes. Follow these exact steps:

  • Step 1: Visit the official government portal at tafcop.sancharsaathi.gov.in. Make sure the URL ends in .gov.in to avoid phishing sites.
  • Step 2: Enter your primary active mobile number in the designated box.
  • Step 3: Fill out the captcha code displayed on the screen and click on 'Validate Captcha'.
  • Step 4: You will receive a One Time Password (OTP) on your mobile number. Enter this OTP and click 'Login'.
  • Step 5: A dashboard will load displaying a list of all mobile numbers that are actively registered under the same ID proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, etc.) that was used to acquire the number you just logged in with.

What to Do if You Spot an Unknown Number

If you see a mobile number on the list that you do not recognize, or a number you previously owned but no longer use, the TAFCOP portal allows you to take immediate action directly from the dashboard.

Select the checkbox next to the suspicious number. You will be given three options:

  • Not my number: Select this if you have never seen or used this number. The DoT will initiate a block and disconnection process with the telecom operator.
  • Not required: Select this if it is an old number of yours that you simply threw away or forgot to formally disconnect.
  • Required: Leave this as-is for the numbers you actively use.

Once you submit a report, you will receive a Ticket ID via SMS, allowing you to track the disconnection status on the portal.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How many SIM cards can a single person own in India?

According to DoT guidelines, a single individual can hold a maximum of 9 active mobile connections across all operators combined. For individuals living in Jammu & Kashmir, Assam, and North East service areas, the limit is 6 connections.

Is the TAFCOP portal safe to use?

Yes. TAFCOP is an official portal operated by the Department of Telecommunications, Government of India. It uses secure OTP authentication and does not ask for your bank details or full Aadhaar number.
