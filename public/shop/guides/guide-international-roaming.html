The Complete Guide to International Roaming (IR) Activation

Traveling outside India with your domestic SIM card can be a daunting experience if you aren't prepared. Without International Roaming (IR) properly configured, your Indian mobile number will show "No Service" the moment you land in a foreign country. This means you will instantly lose access to crucial banking OTPs, credit card transaction alerts, and two-factor authentication for your email accounts.

In this guide, we will explain exactly how to activate International Roaming on Airtel, Jio, and Vodafone Idea (Vi) so you can receive incoming SMS for free and avoid "bill shock" from accidental data usage.

Step 1: Activate the IR Service (Before You Fly)

For most modern prepaid and postpaid connections, the International Roaming service is pre-activated at the account level. However, to ensure your SIM connects to a foreign network partner, it is highly recommended to activate the service manually via your operator's app before boarding your flight.

  • For Airtel: Open the Airtel Thanks app, navigate to the 'Manage' section, click on 'International Roaming', and toggle the IR service to 'ON'.
  • For Jio: Open the MyJio app, go to Settings -> International Roaming, and click 'Activate'. Note: Jio often requires you to purchase an IR pack to latch onto foreign networks.
  • For Vi: Open the Vi App, search for 'International Roaming', and ensure the service is active on your profile.

Step 2: Securing Free Incoming SMS (OTPs)

If you are buying a local tourist SIM card in your destination country for internet data, you only need your Indian SIM active to receive SMS. Incoming SMS is absolutely free on international roaming worldwide for Airtel, Jio, and Vi.

To safely receive OTPs without spending money:

  • Ensure your standard prepaid plan is active (your regular 28-day or 365-day validity plan).
  • Keep your Indian SIM in Slot 1 or as your Primary eSIM.
  • CRITICAL: Go to your phone's settings and turn Data Roaming OFF for your Indian SIM. Background app updates can drain hundreds of rupees in seconds if standard IR data rates apply.
  • Do not answer incoming calls. Incoming voice calls on IR are heavily chargeable unless you have purchased a specific IR voice pack.

Step 3: Purchasing an IR Pack (Optional)

If you intend to use your Indian number to make calls or browse the internet abroad, you must purchase an International Roaming Pack. All three operators offer a variety of packs ranging from 1-day validity to 365-day validity.

Always verify that your destination country is covered under the specific IR pack you are buying. Operators classify countries into different zones, and a pack valid for the USA and UK might not work in parts of Africa or the Middle East.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I use Wi-Fi Calling (VoWiFi) while abroad?

Airtel and Jio allow you to use Wi-Fi calling in certain countries to make calls back to India at local rates. However, you must connect to a secure Wi-Fi network and ensure the VoWiFi symbol is visible before dialing. Check your operator's specific terms, as making calls to the foreign country's local numbers via VoWiFi usually incurs heavy ISD charges.

My phone shows 'No Service' abroad, what should I do?

Go to your phone settings, navigate to Cellular/Mobile Networks, turn off 'Automatic Network Selection', and manually search for networks. Wait for 2-3 minutes for the list to populate, and select a partner network (e.g., AT&T in the US, EE in the UK).
